About Our Valet Trash Service

Potomac Valet Trash partners with multi-family communities to provide consistent doorstep trash pickup on scheduled nights. Residents place bagged trash outside their door, and our team collects it and transports it to the onsite dumpsters—helping keep hallways, breezeways, and common areas cleaner.

Valet trash reduces the burden on maintenance teams, improves property appearance, and elevates the resident experience. We follow your community guidelines (pickup windows, bag limits, and cleanliness standards) and keep communication simple for onsite staff.

Whether you manage a garden-style community or a multi-building property, we tailor the service to match your layout and operating needs.
