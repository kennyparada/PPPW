Valet Trash Service for Apartment Communities

Doorstep pickup that keeps your property cleaner, reduces loose trash, and saves your maintenance team time.

What You Get

  • ✓ Scheduled doorstep pickup (Monday–Friday)
  • ✓ Bagged household trash to on-site dumpsters only
  • ✓ Clear resident guidelines (placement, timing, prohibited items)
  • ✓ Professional, consistent service that protects curb appeal
  • ✓ Simple rollout with signage + communication support

Potomac Valet Trash partners with multi-family communities to provide consistent doorstep trash pickup on scheduled nights. Residents place securely tied, bagged trash outside their door during the service window, and our team collects and transports it to the on-site dumpsters.

The result: fewer overflowing bins, less loose trash in breezeways and common areas, and fewer nightly trash runs for your maintenance team—without adding complexity for onsite staff.

Built for Property Managers

We tailor the route to your building layout, confirm pickup locations and dumpster access, and align on community rules (time window, bag limits, compliance). Missed pickups are handled through a clear process so your office isn’t stuck chasing issues.
