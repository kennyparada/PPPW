What We Handle

Consistent doorstep trash pickup designed for multi-family communities.

Valet Trash Service Scope

  • ✓ Bagged household trash
  • ✓ Doorstep pickup on scheduled nights
  • ✓ Transport to onsite dumpsters or compactors
  • ✓ Hallways, breezeways, and exterior walk-ups
  • ✓ Garden-style and multi-building communities

Potomac Valet Trash provides reliable doorstep trash collection for apartment communities. Residents place properly bagged trash outside their door during designated pickup windows, and our team collects and disposes of it at the property’s designated waste areas.

Our service helps reduce loose trash, keeps common areas cleaner, and minimizes strain on maintenance teams. We follow your community’s rules for pickup times, bag limits, and cleanliness standards to ensure a smooth, compliant operation.
