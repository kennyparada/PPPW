About Our Valet Trash Service

Potomac Valet Trash provides reliable doorstep trash collection for multi-family properties. Residents place properly bagged trash outside their door during a designated window, and our team collects it and disposes of it at your on-site waste area.

Valet trash reduces loose trash in common areas, improves curb appeal, and gives maintenance teams time back for higher-priority work. We follow your community rules for pickup times, bag limits, and cleanliness standards to keep service smooth and consistent.

Multiple buildings, stairwells, or walk-up units? No problem — we tailor routes to your layout without disrupting residents.
