About the Service

Residents place properly bagged household trash outside their door during a designated pickup window. Our team collects it and transports it to your on-site dumpster/compactor area.

Valet trash reduces loose trash in hallways and breezeways, improves property appearance, and gives your maintenance team time back for higher-priority work orders and turns.

Multiple buildings, stairwells, or exterior walk-ups? No problem — we tailor routes to your layout without disrupting residents.

What Residents Do

1) Set out bags

Bagged household trash only, tied and leak-free, placed outside the door during the set window.

2) Follow rules

Follow community rules (bag limits, container rules, prohibited items).

3) Keep area clean

No loose trash, open bags, or spills in front of doors and walkways.

What We Handle

  • Transport to on-site dumpsters/compactors only
  • Routes tailored to building layout (walk-ups, stairwells, breezeways)
