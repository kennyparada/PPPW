About Us

Potomac Valet Trash was built to make apartment living cleaner and easier. We partner with multi-family communities to provide reliable doorstep trash pickup on scheduled nights, helping keep properties tidy, reducing strain on maintenance teams, and improving resident experience.

Our focus is simple: consistent service, clear communication, and professional standards that property managers can depend on.
